The United Nations

The United Nations (UN) is an international organization that brings countries together to promote peace, protect human rights, support development, and solve global problems through cooperation.

  • Established: October 24, 1945
  • Founded after: World War II to help prevent future wars and maintain international peace.
  • Founding members: 51 countries originally joined the UN.
  • UN Day: Celebrated every October 24 to mark the anniversary of the UN's founding.

The 17 Sustainable Development Goals

The 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are global goals established by the United Nations in 2015 to create a better, fairer, and more sustainable world by 2030.

Goal 1

No Poverty

End poverty in all its forms everywhere.

Goal 2

Zero Hunger

End hunger, improve nutrition, and promote sustainable agriculture.

Goal 3

Good Health and Well-Being

Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for everyone.

Goal 4

Quality Education

Provide inclusive and equitable quality education for all.

Goal 5

Gender Equality

Achieve gender equality and empower women.

Goal 6

Clean Water and Sanitation

Ensure access to clean water and proper sanitation.

Goal 7

Affordable and Clean Energy

Ensure access to affordable, reliable, and sustainable energy.

Goal 8

Decent Work and Economic Growth

Promote decent jobs and sustainable economic growth.

Goal 9

Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

Build strong infrastructure and encourage innovation.

Goal 10

Reduced Inequalities

Reduce inequality within and among countries.

Goal 11

Sustainable Cities and Communities

Make cities safe, inclusive, resilient, and sustainable.

Goal 12

Responsible Consumption and Production

Reduce waste and use resources responsibly.

Goal 13

Climate Action

Take urgent action to fight climate change.

Goal 14

Life Below Water

Protect oceans and marine life.

Goal 15

Life on Land

Protect forests, ecosystems, and biodiversity.

Goal 16

Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

Promote peaceful societies and equal access to justice.

Goal 17

Partnerships for the Goals

Strengthen global cooperation to achieve the sustainable development.

The Philippines

The Philippines is an archipelagic country in Southeast Asia known for its rich culture, diverse traditions, and beautiful islands and is one of the founding members of the United Nations (UN).

  • Official Name: Republic of the Philippines
  • Capital City: Manila
  • UN Regional Group: Asia-Pacific Group (APG)
  • UN Admission Date: October 24, 1945
